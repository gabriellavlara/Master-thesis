**Detecting disinformation and narratives in the news using LLM-generated fakes**

While most tools focus on proposing old fact-checks to the current news to support fact-checkers, we want to try to catch new disinformation instances using the generative power of Large Language models.

The goal of the thesis will be to learn if it is possible to detect new disinformation posts by generating potential fakes using Large Language Models based on the news stream and comparing the fresher news for instances with close similarity to the generated instances.

The task will involve creating a dataset for the task, working with both proprietary and open-source LLMs to benchmark them for the task in a pilot project, and identifying whether it can be effective. We will also address ethical questions and explore the adversarial robustness of such a method.
