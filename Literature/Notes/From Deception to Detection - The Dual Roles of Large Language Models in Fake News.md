#LLMs-as-classifiers 
### Their approach:
First generate different fakes using different biases: {age, disability, gener, sexual orientation, religion, etc. }
Then use LLMs to classify authenticity of claim + evaluate clarity and comprehensibility of explanations using human participants -> to analyze effectiveness of LLM-classification in real world scenarios

### May be useful for my work
Interface for models: HuggingChat

Mistral is not trained to follow safety guidelines, which will lead to no reluctance when generating fakes. GPT-4 however has extensive filters.
Some LLMs are somehow more biased towards certain ideas (e.g. social-economic status) than others (e.g. ethnicity and gender).

LLMs show a tendency to be better able to recognize FAKE news than real news. 

Citing fictious studies makes fake less likely to be detected
