#socialmedia #datasets


- People consume news on social media: around 68% of Americans get some of their news on social media in 2018; no regulatory authority
- Characteritstics of fake news datasets:
	- News content
	- Social context
	- Spatiotemporal information (propagation rate)
- Existing datasets:
	- FacebookHoax
Sources for (real-news, fake-claim) pairs:
- (PolitiFact)
- (E! Online, GossipCop)
#### The dataset
- **Fake news dataset with reliable labels annotated by journalists and domain experts**
- multidimensional information from news content, social context and spatiotemporal information
- Datasets of politics and entertainment domain
#### Interesting facts
- most users that spread fake news (around 78%) are still more likely to be humans than bots (around 22%)
**Possible problem:** significant amount of time required to gather tweets using Twitter API
